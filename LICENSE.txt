This repository contains the genome-scale metabolic models iCG875 and eciCG875,
together with the MPECMM (multi-enzyme-constrained metabolic model construction
method) workflow used to derive the enzyme-constrained model eciCG875 from iCG875
(see the models/, doc/ and test/ directories described in README.md).
